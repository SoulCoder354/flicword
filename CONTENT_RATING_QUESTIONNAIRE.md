# Content Rating Questionnaire

## Purpose
This questionnaire is designed to evaluate the suitability of app content for different audiences. It helps assess language, violence, sexual content, references to drugs/alcohol, and overall user safety.

## Questions and Answers

1. **What is the target age group for this app?**
   - Answer: 13+ (teenagers and older). The app uses mild language and educational word-based gameplay suitable for older children and adults.

2. **Does the app contain any profanity or strong language?**
   - Answer: No. The app is designed to avoid profanity and strong language.

3. **Are there any references to violence or physical harm?**
   - Answer: No. The gameplay and content are non-violent and focused on vocabulary, quizzes, and language practice.

4. **Does the app include any sexual content or innuendo?**
   - Answer: No. The content is family-friendly and does not contain sexual themes or mature innuendo.

5. **Are there references to drugs, alcohol, or tobacco?**
   - Answer: No. The app does not promote or reference drugs, alcohol, or tobacco.

6. **Does the app require an internet connection or user-generated content?**
   - Answer: Yes, the app uses online features and may fetch word data or user progress from the backend. User-generated content is limited to profile input and quiz answers.

7. **Is there any gambling or betting content?**
   - Answer: No. The app is focused on educational quizzes and word games without gambling mechanics.

8. **Does the app ask for personal information or access device data?**
   - Answer: The app may require account authentication and basic profile information. It does not request excessive personal data or sensitive device permissions beyond standard app operation.

9. **Is there any content that might be considered scary or disturbing?**
   - Answer: No. The app maintains a positive and non-threatening tone suitable for most audiences.

10. **Does the app contain advertisements or links to external content?**
    - Answer: Not in the core app experience. If external links are present, they are limited to support or legal information and are marked clearly.

## Final Rating Recommendation
- Recommended rating: **Everyone 10+** or **Teen** depending on regional rating systems.
- Summary: The app is primarily educational with vocabulary practice, mild and safe content, and no explicit or mature themes.
